# Product Launch Guidelines

## Pre-Launch Phase (8-12 weeks)
1. **Market Research** (Weeks 1-2)
   - Competitor analysis
   - Customer surveys
   - Market size estimation

2. **Product Development** (Weeks 3-8)
   - Prototype testing
   - Supply chain confirmation
   - Quality assurance

3. **Marketing Preparation** (Weeks 6-12)
   - Campaign development
   - Promotional materials
   - Influencer outreach

## Launch Phase (Weeks 1-4)
- **Week 1**: Exclusive pre-launch for VIP customers
- **Week 2**: Official launch with marketing push
- **Week 3**: Expansion to all channels
- **Week 4**: Assessment and optimization

## Post-Launch Phase
- **Month 2**: Adjust pricing if needed
- **Month 3**: Evaluate performance against targets
- **Month 6**: Conduct customer feedback review
- **Month 12**: Annual performance review

## Success Criteria
- **Sales Target**: 10,000 units in first month
- **Customer Satisfaction**: NPS 45+
- **Return Rate**: Below 5%
- **Repeat Purchase Rate**: Above 20% by month 3

## Budget Allocation
- Product Development: 30%
- Marketing: 40%
- Operations: 20%
- Contingency: 10%
