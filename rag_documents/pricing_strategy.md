# Pricing Strategy

## Core Pricing Approach
RetailMart Global uses value-based pricing with seasonal adjustments to optimize revenue while maintaining competitiveness.

## Pricing Tiers
- **Budget Category**: 5-10% margin
- **Standard Category**: 15-25% margin
- **Premium Category**: 30-40% margin
- **Luxury Category**: 40-50% margin

## Seasonal Adjustments
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar)**: Base pricing + 5%
- **Q2 (Apr-Jun)**: Base pricing (no adjustment)
- **Q3 (Jul-Sep)**: Base pricing - 5%
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Base pricing + 15%

## Competitive Pricing Rules
- Never undercut competitor by more than 10%
- Match competitor pricing within 24 hours
- Premium products maintain 15%+ premium to competitors

## Promotional Pricing
- Limited time offers: Max 2 weeks duration
- Flash sales: Max 48 hours duration
- Clearance: No time limit until sold out

## Price Change Approval
- Changes under $5: Store manager approval
- Changes $5-$20: District manager approval  
- Changes above $20: Regional director approval
