# Inventory Management SOP

## Stock Level Targets
- **Target Safety Stock**: 3 months of average sales
- **Reorder Point**: 1.5 months of average sales
- **Minimum Stock Level**: 0.5 months of average sales

## Regional Adjustments
- **Urban Areas**: 20% reduction in safety stock
- **Rural Areas**: 30% increase in safety stock
- **Seasonal Markets**: Varies by 40-60% based on season

## Reorder Process
1. When stock falls below reorder point, automatic notification
2. Lead time: Standard 14 days from supplier
3. Expedited orders: 3-5 days with 15% premium
4. Emergency orders: 24 hours with 25% premium

## Inventory Audits
- Physical count: Quarterly
- System reconciliation: Monthly
- Full cycle count: Annual

## Obsolescence Policy
- Items with zero sales for 12 months marked for clearance
- 50% discount after 3 months of clearance
- Donation or disposal after 6 months
