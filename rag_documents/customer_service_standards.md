# Customer Service Standards

## Service Level Agreements
- **Critical Issues**: 24 hour resolution time
- **High Priority**: 48 hour resolution time
- **Medium Priority**: 72 hour resolution time
- **Low Priority**: 5 business day resolution time

## NPS (Net Promoter Score) Targets
- **Overall Target**: NPS 50+
- **Segment Targets**:
  - Premium Customers: NPS 60+
  - Regular Customers: NPS 45+
  - New Customers: NPS 40+

## Customer Satisfaction Metrics
- First Contact Resolution (FCR): Target 70%
- Customer Effort Score (CES): Target 2.0 or lower
- Response Time: Target within 2 hours
- Email Response: Target within 4 hours

## Escalation Procedure
- Level 1: Support Agent (can resolve 60% of issues)
- Level 2: Team Lead (can resolve 30% of issues)
- Level 3: Manager (can resolve 8% of issues)
- Level 4: Director (handles 2% of issues)

## Compensation Policy
- Refund without restocking: Orders under $50
- Refund with restocking: Orders $50-$200
- Replacement + discount: Orders above $200
- Credit for future purchase: Option for all refunds
