# Discount Policy

## Overview
RetailMart Global maintains a structured discount policy to ensure revenue optimization while maintaining customer satisfaction.

## Discount Tiers
- **Regular Customers**: Maximum 30% discount
- **Loyal Customers**: Maximum 40% discount  
- **Premium Customers**: Maximum 50% discount

## Approval Requirements
- Discounts up to 25%: No approval needed
- Discounts 25-35%: Manager approval required
- Discounts above 35%: Director approval required

## Special Occasions
- Black Friday: Up to 50% discount allowed
- Christmas: Up to 45% discount allowed
- New Year Sale: Up to 40% discount allowed
- End of Season: Up to 60% clearance allowed

## Restrictions
- No stacking of discounts
- One promotion per transaction
- Minimum order value of $50 for discounts above 30%
