# Financial Targets & KPIs

## Revenue Targets
- **Annual Revenue Growth**: 8-12% YoY
- **Quarterly Growth**: 2-3% QoQ
- **Same-Store Sales Growth**: 5-8% YoY

## Profitability Targets
- **Gross Margin Target**: 35-40%
- **Operating Margin Target**: 12-18%
- **Net Profit Margin Target**: 5-8%
- **EBITDA Margin**: 15-20%

## Return on Investment (ROIC)
- **Target ROIC**: 15%
- **Cost of Capital**: 8%
- **Value Creation Spread**: 7% minimum

## Regional Performance Benchmarks
- **Tier 1 Stores** (Metro): 15% profit margin target
- **Tier 2 Stores** (Urban): 12% profit margin target
- **Tier 3 Stores** (Rural): 10% profit margin target

## Cost Management
- **COGS Target**: 60-65% of revenue
- **SG&A Target**: 20-25% of revenue
- **Labor Cost**: 8-10% of revenue
- **Occupancy Cost**: 5-7% of revenue

## Cash Flow Targets
- **Operating Cash Flow**: 50%+ of Net Income
- **Free Cash Flow**: 30%+ of Net Income
- **Inventory Turnover**: 6-8 times per year
- **Days Inventory Outstanding**: 45-60 days
